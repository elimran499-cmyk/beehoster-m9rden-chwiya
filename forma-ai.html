Frequently asked

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, and your projects stay exactly where they are.

What happens to my work if I cancel?

Everything you have already exported is yours to keep, forever. Cloud projects stay readable on the Free plan so you can download them at any time.

Do you offer team and education discounts?

Teams of five or more get volume pricing on Pro, and verified students and educators get Standard at no cost for twelve months.
